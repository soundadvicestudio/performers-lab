Vocal Coach & Music Director · Sound Advice Vocal Studio

I've spent years in rehearsal rooms and in the wings — coaching singers, directing music, and watching what separates performers who grow from those who plateau. My approach is grounded in the Biopsychosocial model: your voice is shaped by biology, psychology, and environment, and great coaching honors all three.

The Performer's Lab exists because consistent, specific feedback is the single most reliable driver of growth — and it shouldn't require weekly private-lesson budgets to access it.
